# VOIDZONE — MULTI-RESOURCE BOOKING UX GUIDE (`v16.0`)

## 1. Fast Path vs Smart Multi-Resource Builder
- **Quick Booking (1 Station)**: Pick Branch → Time Window → Tap 1 Station (or leave on `ANY`) → Click **Pay at desk** or **Pay from wallet**. Completes in under 10 seconds.
- **Multi-Resource Selection (Same or Mixed Types)**:
  - Tap any combination of stations on the **2D Mazgaon Arena Map** (`PC-1`, `PC-2`, `PS5-1`, `SIM-1`). Every clicked station highlights with an orange border, `✓` badge, and live price (`+₹160`, `+₹200`, `+₹400`).
  - Tap multiple **Pre-Warm Games** (`Valorant`, `Counter-Strike 2`, `GTA V`, `EA Sports FC`, `Gran Turismo 7`, `VR Boxing`) — every selected game pill lights up with a glowing orange frosted-glass capsule.
- **Per-Resource Durations & Combo Packages**:
  - Inside the **Smart Multi-Resource Builder**, adjust individual durations per selected station (e.g., `PC-1 → 2 hrs`, `PS5-1 → 1 hr`, `SIM-1 → 30 min`) or click a 1-Click Combo Package (`PC Duo Pack`, `Friends 4-Pack`, `5v5 LAN Squad Pack`, `Hybrid Squad Pack`, `Apex Sim + PC Combo`, `Private Group Session`).
- **Post-Booking Management**:
  - Each confirmed ticket supports **+ Next Slot**, **🔁 +1 Rig**, **📅 Reschedule +1h**, **📥 .ICS Calendar**, **✓ Staff Check In**, and **✕ Cancel** (with automatic Wallet refund for prepaid bookings).
