# VOIDZONE — New Design Direction: Spatial Obsidian & Precision Glass (`Section 4`)

## 1. Core Aesthetic Vision
The `v13.0` VOIDZONE interface replaces cyberpunk wireframe clichés with **Spatial Obsidian & Precision Glass** — a calm, high-contrast, gaming-native visual language built around:
- **Deep Obsidian Foundation (`#06070b`)**: Maximizes legibility for live station telemetry, tournament brackets, and creator metrics.
- **Precision Frosted Glass Where It Matters**: Glass blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`) is reserved for floating navigation (`header`, `FrostedGlassTabBar`, `.vz-ios-subtab-bar`, drawers) while content cards use high-contrast `--surface-default` (`rgba(13, 16, 23, 0.88)`) and `--surface-elevated` (`rgba(19, 23, 33, 0.92)`).
- **Controlled Brand Energy (`#ff6200` to `#f43f5e`)**: Warm VOIDZONE orange and crimson communicate active selection, primary actions, and live availability rather than decorating every static border.

## 2. 3-Second & 10-Second Hierarchy Standard (`Section 37`)
- **Within 3 Seconds**: Users immediately see where they are (active `.vz-liquid-lens` pill), live arena availability (`22 of 22 stations free`), and the primary action (`Book a Rig`).
- **Within 10 Seconds**: Users understand the connected VOIDZONE ecosystem (`Book`, `Community · Social · Creators · Collab · Esports`, `Cooler`, `Rewards`) and can act in one click.
